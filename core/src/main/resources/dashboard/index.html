All circuits

All circuits lists every circuit from both panels, sorted by usage, name or room (rooms are set with rooms in the bridge's config). As wired draws each panel as it is wired: odd spaces on the left, even on the right, two-pole breakers spanning two rows. The graph behind each breaker is its draw over the last 24 hours, in 15-minute averages from Prometheus, scaled to that breaker's own peak; the dot is the current reading. Turning a circuit off takes two taps, because it cuts power at the breaker. Locked circuits are ones the panel won't switch. Tap a circuit for its usage over the last 30 days; tap its switch to turn it on or off. Circuits hidden from Home appear, dimmed, only in the wired view.
