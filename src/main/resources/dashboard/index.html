Panel

    Odd spaces on the left, even on the right, as in the panel; two-pole breakers span two rows. The graph behind each breaker is its draw over the last 24 hours, in 15-minute averages from Prometheus, scaled to that breaker's own peak; the dot is the current reading. Turning a circuit off takes two taps, because it cuts power at the breaker. Locked circuits are ones the panel won't switch; dimmed ones are hidden from Home.
